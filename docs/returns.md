# Returns and exchanges

Sales create an RMA against an original invoice and an actually delivered shipment line. Managed shipments must be Delivered; dispatch alone is not delivery. Warehouse staff can confirm delivery evidence for older direct shipments that have no managed delivery. The RMA preserves original invoice/order/shipment references and product, size and color identification. Reasons include incorrect size/color/delivery, manufacturing defects, customer cancellation and other reasons.

The current invoicing workflow creates an invoice only after an order is fully dispatched. A delivered portion of an order cannot therefore be returned through this workflow until its original invoice exists.

Open return requests reserve eligibility, not stock. The cumulative requested quantities of open authorizations plus actual quantities received on closed intakes cannot exceed that delivered shipment line. Rejection/cancellation is allowed before receipt and releases eligibility. Partial intake can be closed after every received unit is inspected; unreceived authorized units then become returnable again. Original shipping counters remain historical and are never decreased by an RMA.

Sales approve the request. Warehouse records physical receipts with unique document references; these goods are held pending inspection and do not enter available finished stock. Duplicate operation keys are idempotent, duplicate receipt references are rejected, and quantities cannot exceed authorization. Record the physically observed SKU when the reason is an incorrect size/color/delivery. Otherwise the original SKU is the default. Inspection and quarantine disposition post the actual returned SKU, not an invented quantity of the originally ordered SKU.

Finished stock is tracked by variant, not by customer branding. Inspectors must quarantine customer-branded returns unless they have verified that the items are suitable for general resale or have been debranded.

Quality/inventory staff inspect each receipt once, accounting for every unit as sellable or quarantined. Only the sellable portion of a sellable-condition inspection posts a stock increase. Defective/non-sellable goods remain in a separate quarantine ledger, outside available inventory. Explicit documented reinspection can release quarantine to available stock; a scrap document records disposal without a stock increase. Inspection, receipt and disposition history are immutable. Commercial completion does not automatically release quarantine.

Sales request one resolution for each inspected unit: Credit, Refund, Exchange or Replacement. Pending requests hold those units against competing resolutions. Administrators approve/reject commercial requests and process approved decisions. Rejected pending requests release their commercial allocation; issued documents cannot be rewritten.

Credits use the original agreed invoice amounts, including product discounts, branding charges and tax. Invoice value is allocated deterministically among sales lines, then cumulative unit proration allocates partial-credit rounding cents. Credits cannot exceed original units or invoice value. Final amounts are fixed when credit notes are issued; earlier request values are estimates. Original invoice total and gross payment history are retained. Receivable = max(original invoice − issued credits − gross payments + recorded refunds, 0). Any surplus is the customer's refundable credit balance. Payment entry, invoice views, dashboard outstanding and invoice CSV exports use the adjusted balance.

Refund processing requires a settled invoice and a reference to an externally executed bank/cash refund; no bank transfer is initiated by this module. For unpaid invoices, issue a credit against the debt. A credit on an already paid invoice leaves a refundable balance until an administrator records the external refund. Duplicate refund references and over-refunds are rejected. Amounts remain in the original invoice currency, with no FX conversion or cross-invoice credit-wallet transfer.

Exchange/replacement processing creates a linked, approved no-charge draft sales order. Replacement uses the original variant; a size/color exchange uses another variant of the same product. No shipment or inventory deduction occurs automatically. Reserve and dispatch the new order through normal fulfillment. Any required manufacturing uses the new variant's normal approved BOM/specification. Originally branded products require branding approval for the new sales line; customer artwork/proof approval is not silently reused. Price-difference exchanges use a separate normal quotation/credit workflow rather than hidden repricing.

An RMA completes when intake is closed, all received units are inspected and every received unit has a processed commercial resolution. Audit events retain request, authorization, receipt, inspection, quarantine, credit/refund and replacement decisions. Sales manage authorization and commercial requests; inventory receives; inventory/production quality roles inspect; administrators issue commercial resolutions/refunds. Viewer access to RMA pages is denied.
