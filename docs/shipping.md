# Shipping and delivery

Reserve finished goods on a confirmed sales order, then open **Picking lists, shipments and delivery history**. Inventory staff create a delivery with the customer's requested address, carrier, vehicle, driver, tracking reference and the quantities to pick. Customer and product descriptions are retained as shipment snapshots.

Planned quantities commit part of the sales line's existing reservation; they do not create another inventory reservation or movement. The sum of open picking commitments cannot exceed reserved goods. The previous direct-shipment action is blocked from consuming goods committed to a picking list. Multiple deliveries may fulfill one sales order, including partial shipments.

Record cumulative picked and packed quantities for each line. Packed ≤ picked ≤ planned. All lines must be fully packed before dispatch. A packed, undispatched delivery can reopen picking for corrections; cancel/recreate if the planned assignment itself must change. Picking and packing do not deduct stock. The printed list includes every line, without pagination, customer address, carrier/vehicle/driver information and recipient signature fields.

**Confirm physical dispatch** creates the existing shipment/line records, deducts on-hand and reserved inventory, updates reserved/shipped sales quantities and recalculates sales fulfillment in one transaction. Operation-key retries and subsequent dispatch attempts cannot post stock twice. Shipment numbers are stable; historical direct shipments remain in the shipping history. Invoices continue to use the existing agreed sales amounts and shipped-order workflow.

Cancellation is available only before dispatch. It releases the picking commitment, keeping the sales reservation intact for a replacement delivery, and makes no inventory movement. After dispatch, record In Transit, Delivered or Exception and a note; cancellation cannot restore goods. A physical return requires a separate explicit return process rather than a shipping status change. Delivered/cancelled documents and event history are immutable. Physical delivery status is independent of warehouse fulfillment and invoice payment status.

Attach signed PNG/JPEG/PDF proof after dispatch. Files are limited to 2 MiB, signature-validated and SHA-256 checked. Private Vercel Blob storage requires BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN connected to a private store. The application never uses public attachment URLs. Reads go through authenticated handlers and verify delivery ownership and file integrity. Evidence/history is retained rather than deleted; duplicate proof hashes are rejected and uncommitted uploads cleaned up. If storage is unavailable, upload controls are disabled or return an explicit configuration error.

Inventory roles manage picking, packing, dispatch, tracking and proof uploads. Sales roles can read delivery reports and proof. Other roles cannot access shipping pages or file routes. All stock actions use the existing audit/idempotency transaction wrapper. Delivery reports show picked, packed and dispatched quantities, carrier/tracking, timestamps and legacy shipping history; detail pages link to sales, customer and invoices.
